Evening reset, five steps

Do this week

One room, five moves, done before dinner. The lamp is on and there is a place to put a bowl.

Evening reset for the room you sit in

Do this in the room where you actually spend the night. Not the guest room. You need about 15 minutes.

  1. Turn off the overhead light. Turn on one lamp at the height of your eyes when you are sitting. If the bulb is harsh, use a lower watt or a warmer one you already own.
  2. Put the throw you already have on the chair or the sofa corner you use. Fold it once so it looks intentional and you can grab it.
  3. Clear the table or the coffee table down to one thing: a book, a branch in a jar, or nothing. Mail goes in another room.
  4. If you want a smell, simmer orange peel and one cinnamon stick in a small pot of water while you are in the kitchen. Stay nearby. Turn it off before you leave the room.
  5. Unroll your yoga mat where you can see it, or skip this if you already practiced. Set a glass of water on the table. Sit down.

That is the whole how-to. You do not need new pillows. If the room is cold, close the window or put on a sweater before you call it cozy.
