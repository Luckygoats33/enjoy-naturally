Pinecone bird feeders

A feeder you can hang before dark. Skip nuts if anyone in the house has a nut allergy. Use sunflower seed butter or another seed butter you already know is safe for your people.

Makes 4 feedersAbout 30 minutesPlus drying time

Materials

  • 4 open pinecones
  • 1/2 cup sunflower seed butter, or another seed butter that fits your household
  • 1 cup birdseed
  • Kitchen string or twine
  • A plate or tray
  • Wax paper, if you want the table clean

Steps

  1. Tie a loop of string around the top of each pinecone, long enough to hang.
  2. Spread a thin coat of seed butter on the scales. A thick glob falls off and can be a problem for little kids who still put hands in their mouths. Thin is the point.
  3. Pour birdseed on the plate. Roll each cone until the butter holds a coat of seed.
  4. Set them on wax paper for 20 minutes so the coat sets.
  5. Hang them from a branch you can see, higher than a dog can reach. Bring them in at night if seed on the ground is a problem where you live.

Wash hands after. This is a craft, not a snack. For what to eat afterward, use the plates in the family snack guide. Cut round foods for little children. That guidance does not change because the project was cute.
