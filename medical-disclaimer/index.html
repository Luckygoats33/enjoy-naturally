Medical disclaimer

Enjoy Naturally is a lifestyle journal. It is not a medical, dietetic, or mental-health service, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified clinician.

Not advice for your body

Stories about food, herbs, sleep, movement, and family routines are general information. They are not personalized advice. They are not meant to diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, or manage any disease or condition. Do not change a medication, a prescribed diet, or a care plan because of something you read here. Talk with your physician, pharmacist, or other licensed clinician about your own situation.

Food is not medicine on this site

We may discuss what a process does to fruit, how a label uses the word “natural,” or how to pack a lunch that stays cold. Those are kitchen and safety topics. They are not claims that a snack will change a lab result, a diagnosis, or a child's development. Foods here are foods. Nothing on this blog says a snack, recipe, or ingredient treats autism or any other condition.

Herbs and traditions

When we write about herbs, we write about taste, growing, and culinary or cultural tradition. Traditional use is not the same thing as evidence that an herb treats an illness. People who are pregnant, nursing, giving herbs to children, or taking medication should ask a clinician before using herbs beyond ordinary amounts in food.

Allergies, choking, and food safety

Family stories repeat public guidance from agencies such as the CDC and USDA on choking hazards and temperature. That guidance is general. Allergy decisions, swallowing concerns, and what a specific child can eat safely belong with that child's clinician. In an emergency, including a choking episode where someone cannot breathe, call your local emergency number.

If you need care

If you think you have a medical emergency, contact emergency services. For ongoing symptoms, see a licensed health professional. Enjoy Naturally cannot reply as a clinic, and the contact form is not a way to get medical advice.

Yoga included

Stretching, walking, and a calmer evening are hobbies I write about. They are not treatment for anxiety, insomnia, injury, or any other condition. A sequence that feels fine in my living room may be wrong for your knee. I cannot see you. Do not replace care you already have with a post. If movement hurts in a way that worries you, stop and ask a licensed clinician.

Food included

A recipe is dinner. An explainer about dried fruit is a kitchen lesson. Neither one is a plan to change your labs, your medication, or a child's development. I will not say a food boosts immunity, detoxes you, or stands in for sleep. Herbs are flavor and tradition here. People who are pregnant, nursing, giving herbs to children, or taking medication should ask a clinician before using herbs as more than ordinary food.

If this is urgent

If you think you are having a medical emergency, contact emergency services. If someone is choking and cannot breathe or speak, call your local emergency number. Do not write the contact form and wait. I will not see it as a clinician, because I am not one. A note left on this page stays in your browser.

The editorial disclosure explains how I handle sources. This page explains the limit of what a lifestyle blog is allowed to be. I intend to stay inside that limit.
