Sheet-pan squash and chickpeas

Dinner in about 45 minutes. The edges brown, and you only wash one pan.

Serves 415 minutes prep30 minutes in the oven

Ingredients

  • 1 small butternut squash (about 2 pounds), peeled, seeded, and cut in 3/4-inch cubes
  • 1 can (15 ounces) chickpeas, drained and patted dry
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves, or 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • Black pepper
  • Plain yogurt, for serving, if you want it

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment if you want an easier cleanup.
  2. Toss the squash and chickpeas with the oil, salt, thyme, and pepper.
  3. Spread them in one layer. Crowding steams them.
  4. Roast 25 to 30 minutes, until the squash is tender and the chickpeas are browned in spots. Stir once halfway.
  5. Taste for salt. Spoon into bowls. Add yogurt if you are using it.

Cabbage and apple skillet

A side or a light dinner in 25 minutes, built to use a soft apple.

Serves 2About 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 small green cabbage, shredded
  • 1 apple, cored and sliced
  • 1 tablespoon butter or olive oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar, optional

Steps

  1. Warm the butter or oil in a wide skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add the cabbage and salt. Cook, stirring, until it wilts and picks up color, about 10 minutes.
  3. Add the apple. Cook until the apple is tender and the cabbage tastes sweet at the edges, about 8 minutes more.
  4. Pepper it. Add the vinegar if you want a sharper bite. Taste for salt and eat.

Baked apples with maple oats

Dessert or tomorrow's breakfast. Two apples, one small dish.

Serves 210 minutes prep30 to 35 minutes in the oven

Ingredients

  • 2 apples
  • 2 tablespoons rolled oats
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon butter, cut in two bits
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tablespoons water

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Core the apples without going all the way through the bottom. Set them in a small baking dish.
  3. Stir the oats, maple, cinnamon, and salt. Pack that into the apples. Top each with a bit of butter.
  4. Pour the water around them, not over the filling.
  5. Bake 30 to 35 minutes, until the apples slump when you press them. Rest 5 minutes, then eat.

Apple oat crisp with maple

A pan of crisp for tonight. Sweet from the apples and the maple, not from a long ingredient list.

Serves 615 minutes prep40 minutes in the oven

Ingredients

  • 4 apples, sliced (peel them if you want; I often leave the peel)
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 tablespoons maple syrup
  • 4 tablespoons cold butter, cut in pieces
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Butter a 2-quart baking dish, or a dish about 8 inches square.
  2. Toss the apples with 1 tablespoon maple syrup and the lemon juice. Spread them in the dish.
  3. Stir the oats, flour, cinnamon, and salt. Work in the butter with your fingers until you have clumps. Stir in the remaining 3 tablespoons maple syrup.
  4. Scatter the topping over the apples.
  5. Bake 40 minutes, until the topping is browned and the apples bubble at the edge. Cool 10 minutes before you scoop it.
