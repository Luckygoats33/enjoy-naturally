Cooler Room Flow, 8 minutes

Why: your shoulders drop and you are done before the tea cools. You need a mat or a blanket, and enough space to kneel. Take the sweater off once you are warm. This is a stretch sequence, not treatment for pain, anxiety, or sleep.

  1. 1 minute, on your back. Hug your knees. If the floor is cold, fold a blanket under you first.
  2. 1 minute, tabletop. Hands under shoulders, knees under hips. Round the back, then flatten it, five slow breaths. Cat and cow, small range.
  3. 1 minute, child's pose. Hips toward heels, arms forward or stacked under your forehead. Skip this if your knees complain, and sit on your heels on a cushion instead.
  4. 2 minutes, low lunge, one each side. Step one foot forward. Back knee down. Hands on blocks, books, or the floor. You should feel a stretch in the front of the back hip, not a pinch in the knee.
  5. 1 minute, standing fold. Feet hip-width, knees soft. Hang. Do not lock the knees to look straight.
  6. 2 minutes, twist on your back. One minute each side. Knees fall together to one side, arms open, and switch.

Stop if something hurts in a joint. Eight minutes is the whole practice. Put your socks back on. Drink water. If you want a longer walk instead, the fall walk list is the outdoor version of the same idea.
