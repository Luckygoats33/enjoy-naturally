Outside, even when it is a short trip

I am not a backcountry person in these essays. I am a person who owns a coat and forgets her gloves. Outdoors, on this blog, means the block, the park, a cold morning, and the bird feeder I can see from the window. If you want a summit, there are better guides. If you want permission to count a twenty-minute walk, you are in the right place.

I write what the air felt like and what I wore. I do not turn a walk into a wellness protocol. Being outside is something I like. It is not a treatment.

Walks I repeat

  • The loop. Same streets, different coat. I leave the podcast off for the first few minutes so I notice the light.
  • After rain. I go anyway if it is only damp. I turn around if it is the kind of cold that makes me angry. There is no prize for misery.
  • With someone small. A scavenger hunt of three things: a color, a sound, something soft. The printable version is still being drawn so the type is large enough. The spoken version works today.
  • The feeder check. Did the pinecone stay hung. Did anything eat. That is a complete outdoor errand.

What I bring

A snack that will not melt into a tragedy, water, and a layer I can take off. The snacking piece is what I pack when the walk is longer than the block. Gloves live by the door now, which is the most useful outdoors tip I have.

Beginner is not an insult here. If you are learning the names of three trees on your street, write them down. I am still doing that. Cold fingers are part of the story, not a failure of character.

Cooler weather, outside

I leave earlier because the light leaves earlier. I wear the coat before I am officially cold, because turning back for it ruins the walk. Gloves by the door. A snack that will not freeze into a brick if I am only gone twenty minutes, which I usually am.

After rain, the leaves smell like the good part of autumn and also like slippery pavement. I watch my feet. I do not need a scenic overlook. The block in a wool hat is the whole essay some days. If I collect something, it is a few leaves for the table and then I go inside while I am still glad I went.
