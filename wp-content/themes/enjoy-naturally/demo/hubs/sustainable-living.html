One week of eating what you already bought. The bin stays lighter because dinner did.

Fall food-waste week

Why: the crisper is where autumn goes to die. This is seven dinners and snacks that use that food. No new gadgets. Shop only if you are actually out of the thing the day names.

  1. Monday, soup. Onion, any soft vegetable, and the wild rice soup method if you have mushrooms and rice. If you do not, simmer what you have in salted water, and eat it with bread.
  2. Tuesday, squash. Roast what is left using the sheet-pan method. Eat it with eggs if you are out of chickpeas.
  3. Wednesday, apple. The softest apple goes into the cabbage skillet or the baked apples.
  4. Thursday, herbs. Cut the limp herbs into dinner. Thyme in the pot, parsley on the bowl. See five herbs if you need the amounts.
  5. Friday, lunch from last night. Pack the leftover in a container while it is still cooling, with an ice pack if it leaves the fridge. Do not leave it on the counter to "cool all afternoon."
  6. Saturday, one snack you finish. Yogurt and fruit, or apple and cheese, from the snack plates. Eat it sitting down so the rest does not disappear into the couch.
  7. Sunday, the scrap pot. Onion ends, squash peels that are clean, and herb stems. Cover with water, simmer 30 minutes, strain, and use the liquid for the next soup or toss it if you will not. Then stop.

Cool leftovers within 2 hours and reheat until steaming. The USDA explains the temperature range on its Danger Zone page. Missing a day is fine. Repeat Monday.
