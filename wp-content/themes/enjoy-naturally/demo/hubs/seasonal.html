I write the month I am standing in

November is the month on the homepage because it is the month I am in. Soup. Leaves. A shorter walk. Yoga that happens on the rug because the studio feels far. I do not write a generic "live well" essay and pretend it fits July. The season is the assignment.

This page is the hub for that. Some stories are finished and filed under kitchen or family. Some are still notes I will not pretend are recipes yet. Both belong here, labeled honestly.

What November asks of me

  • The pot. Wild rice and mushroom soup is the dinner I trust. It simmers while I answer the day.
  • The snack. Apples, yogurt, a measured handful of something dried. The snacking notes are the longer version.
  • The mat. Ten minutes is a real practice. I wrote the shape of it on the mindful living page.
  • The outside. A loop around the block in a coat. Collect a few leaves if a kid, or the kid in me, wants a project.
  • The room. A lamp instead of the overhead. Branches in a jar if I walked past something pretty.

Still on my list

Natural fall decor, a leaf crown, and a scavenger hunt are drafted in my notes and not finished as stories. I would rather show you the idea than invent a tutorial I have not tried twice. The kitchen pieces are ahead of them on purpose. People can eat a soup tonight. A craft can wait for a free Saturday.

When the month turns, this page turns with it. If you are reading in another season, the method still holds: cook what is here, move a little, and do not ask January to feel like June.

A November list I can actually do

  • Soup once, leftovers twice.
  • One baked apple night.
  • Yoga in socks if the floor is rude.
  • A walk while there is still light, even ten minutes.
  • Leaves on the beds or in a bowl, not in a performance.
  • Lamp on by late afternoon so the room does not turn harsh.
  • Text a friend the recipe instead of saving it in a folder I will not open.

Fall is allowed to be ordinary. The pretty parts are the squash, the coats, and the earlier dark. I write those down and I let the rest be a normal week.
