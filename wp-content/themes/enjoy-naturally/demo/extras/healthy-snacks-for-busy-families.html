What I mean when I pack for other people

I wrote this for afternoons when I am feeding people who are in a hurry, including kids who are not mine and kids who are somebody's whole world. The pattern stays small: produce, something that lingers, and a cold pack if the food can spoil. I do not grade the lunchbox. A packaged bar in the car is a day, not a verdict.

If you are the adult who knows a child's allergies, swallowing, and what their clinician said, you outrank me. Use the public links above for the general hazards, and use your own knowledge for the specific child. I would rather you skip a suggestion than stretch it to fit a kid it was not written for.
