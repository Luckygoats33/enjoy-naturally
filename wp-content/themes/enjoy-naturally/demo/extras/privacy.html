The short version

This is a personal blog. I am not running an ad network, and I do not want your medical history. The only information I am interested in is what you choose to type into a form: an email for a future letter, or a note you want me to read.

Where a form note goes

On the WordPress site, newsletter and contact notes are stored for me in the dashboard, and the site tries to email the administrator. Saving an address does not subscribe you and does not send you a confirmation. I do not sell those notes. I do not want passwords or clinical details in that box.

What I do not do

  • I do not sell addresses.
  • I do not buy a list and pretend you asked to hear from me.
  • I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Family stories are written for adults.
  • I do not use the contact form as a medical intake. It is not one.

If analytics are added later, this page will name the tool before it is turned on. A link from a story to a government site is not me adopting that site's privacy policy. It is a citation.
