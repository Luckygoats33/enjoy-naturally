The four I repeat

The list of ten is honest, and I still only rotate four. Yogurt and fruit. An apple and cheese. Cucumbers and hummus. A scooped mix from a jar. I wrote them on a card inside the cabinet because on a loud day I will not invent a fifth. If your four are different, good. The point is that they are foods you recognize, in a portion you chose on purpose.

I snack in a chair when I can. Five quiet minutes tells me whether I wanted food or a pause. Some days the answer is both. That is allowed. This is not a moral system. It is how I stop standing over the sink pretending I am not eating.
