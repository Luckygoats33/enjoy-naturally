Yoga included

Stretching, walking, and a calmer evening are hobbies I write about. They are not treatment for anxiety, insomnia, injury, or any other condition. A sequence that feels fine in my living room may be wrong for your knee. I cannot see you. Do not replace care you already have with a post. If movement hurts in a way that worries you, stop and ask a licensed clinician.

Food included

A recipe is dinner. An explainer about dried fruit is a kitchen lesson. Neither one is a plan to change your labs, your medication, or a child's development. I will not say a food boosts immunity, detoxes you, or stands in for sleep. Herbs are flavor and tradition here. People who are pregnant, nursing, giving herbs to children, or taking medication should ask a clinician before using herbs as more than ordinary food.

If this is urgent

If you think you are having a medical emergency, contact emergency services. If someone is choking and cannot breathe or speak, call your local emergency number. Do not write the contact form and wait. I will not see it as a clinician, because I am not one, and on the preview I may not see it at all.
