A personal blog, said plainly

Enjoy Naturally is written in the first person because it is one person's blog. Recipes, yoga, and the rest of ordinary life. I am not a secret review desk for a company, and I am not going to invent a parent brand in the footer. If that ever changed, this page would change in the same week, in plain language, above the fold of the disclosure.

What counts as a fact here

When I describe how freeze-drying works, what a lunchbox temperature rule is, or how a label uses a word, I am making a factual claim. Those get a link to a public source I can stand behind: a government page, a university extension, or a document that says the thing I said. When I describe what I like, that is taste. Taste does not get disguised as a study.

I would rather cut a number than guess one. If I am unsure, the sentence does not ship.

Products, ads, and the word "partner"

This blog is not an affiliate machine. If I use a product, I say I used it. If someone pays me, or gives me something, to mention them, the story will say that before you are asked to care. A mention is never permission to imply the thing treats, cures, or prevents a condition. I will not take that kind of partnership.

Corrections and tools

If a factual error is more than a typo, I correct the story and note the change. Write me through the contact page. I may use software to help draft. I still decide what I am willing to publish. Software does not get a byline, and it does not get to invent a source.
