A week in this blog

If you are new, this is the shape of it. I cook, I get on a mat or I walk, I notice the season, and I write it down so I can find it later. Some weeks the best thing I make is toast. I publish that kind of week too, because a blog that only shows the soup on its best night is a catalog, not a life.

You will see recipes with times from my stove. You will see yoga that fits between the sofa and the table. You will see family ideas for afternoons that do not need a reservation. You will not see a challenge that shames you for falling off by Wednesday.

What I owe you when I publish

If I state a fact about food safety, a label, or how a process works, I link a public source. If I am only telling you what I like, I say it is what I like. Those are different sentences. Mixing them up is how blogs become advertisements. I do not want that.

The kitchen, the yoga and lifestyle notes, and the month I am in are the doors. Come in wherever you are hungry.
