A journal from Nature's Turn

enjoy naturally

Live well · Eat real · Enjoy more

Enjoy Naturally is a journal about seasonal food, home, garden, and family life. The work is practical on purpose: what to cook, what to notice outside, and how to read a label without handing the decision to a slogan.

A Nature's Turn publication.

Enjoy Naturally is published by Nature's Turn. Nature's Turn makes freeze-dried fruit and other simple foods. This journal is the editorial side of that work. We will not pretend the publication and the company are strangers.

Nature's Turn is led by Will Wiedenmann. When a story mentions a Nature's Turn product, or when the business relationship matters to the piece, we say so in the story itself. A product mention is never a promise about health, and it is never a review unless we label it as one.

What we cover

Food and recipes, the garden, herbs as cooking and tradition, the natural home, family life, mindful everyday habits, the season, time outdoors, and living with less waste. Freeze-dried fruit is one thread. It is not the whole cloth.

What we will not claim

We do not say a food treats, cures, prevents, or manages a condition. We do not say it boosts immunity, detoxes the body, or stands in for sleep, medicine, or a clinician. Herbs, when we write about them, are framed as flavor and tradition. Questions about health belong with a qualified professional. The medical disclaimer is the longer version of this paragraph.

How a story gets here

Stories are edited before they are published. Facts that can be checked are tied to a public source, usually a government page or a university extension. If we get something wrong, write through the contact page and we will correct the record. Ownership, affiliates, and corrections are spelled out in the editorial disclosure.

Photographs

Photographs in this first version were created for Enjoy Naturally. They are not pictures of our readers, our staff, or a specific family's kitchen. Later stories may use photographs from cooks, gardeners, and makers, and those will be credited in the caption.

Say hello

Story ideas, corrections, and partnership notes go through the contact form. The seasonal letter, when the list is connected, will live on the newsletter page.
