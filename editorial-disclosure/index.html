Disclosure

Editorial disclosure

Readers should be able to tell who publishes Enjoy Naturally, how a story is sourced, and when a business interest is in the room.

Ownership

Enjoy Naturally is published by Nature's Turn. Nature's Turn makes freeze-dried fruit and related foods, and is led by Will Wiedenmann. That relationship is stated in the header, the footer, and on the about page. The standing line on our stories is: Enjoy Naturally is published by Nature's Turn.

Independence, stated honestly

Stories are written for readers first: to explain a process, share a recipe, or make a season easier to live in. Nature's Turn owns the publication, so we do not describe the journal as an outside reviewer of our own company. Product mentions are rare. When they happen, they are labeled in the story and they are not health claims.

Sources

Factual statements about food processing, labels, nutrition data, and food safety cite a public source. We prefer U.S. government pages, such as FDA, USDA, and CDC, and university extension publications. A source is linked from the story. If a number is uncertain, we leave the number out rather than guess.

Health language

We do not claim that a food or herb treats, cures, prevents, or manages a disease or condition. We do not claim it boosts immunity or detoxifies the body. The medical disclaimer explains the limit of what a lifestyle journal can say.

Affiliates and sponsors

This preview has no affiliate links and no sponsored stories. If either appears later, the story or link will say so in plain language before a reader is asked to click.

Corrections

If we publish an error, we will correct the story and note the change when the error is more than a typo. Send corrections through the contact page.

Editing tools

Editors may use software to help draft a story. A person reviews every story before it is published. Software does not decide what Enjoy Naturally stands behind.

Photographs

Images in this first version were created for the journal. They do not document a real reader's home. Captions on article pages say so. Future photographs of people will be used with permission and credit.
