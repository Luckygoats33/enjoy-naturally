Take this list outside. Ten things, one loop, then you are done.

Fall walk, 10 things

Why: you get out while there is still light, and you know when the walk is over. Wear a coat before you are officially cold. Gloves if you own them. Twenty minutes is enough.

  1. One yellow leaf, still on a tree or already down.
  2. One brown leaf that crunches when you step.
  3. Something soft: moss, a seed head, or your own sleeve.
  4. A sound you can name: a car, a bird, wind, your shoes.
  5. A smell that is not your coat. Wet pavement counts.
  6. A door or window with a warm light already on.
  7. One thing a bird could use: a berry, a cone, a shrub.
  8. Your shadow, if the sun is low enough to make one.
  9. A place you could sit for ten seconds, even if you do not.
  10. The turnaround: the corner where you head home.

Print this page or screenshot the list. Check items in your head. You do not need a scenic view. When you hit item 10, go inside. If you want food after, make baked apples or heat a bowl of soup.
